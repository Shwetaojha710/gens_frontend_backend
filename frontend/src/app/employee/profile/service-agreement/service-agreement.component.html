The Foundation Training (FT)

The FT may consist of several training modules (hereinafter the terms “module” and “programme” are used interchangeably).

Currently there are two training modules, namely:

a) Technical Training; and

b) Management Skills Training

Technical Training further comprises of 2 distinct modules

a) Generic Stream; and

b) Technology Specific Stream

All references to “Technical Training” shall include both Generic Stream and Technology Specific Stream.

Technical Training:

The Engineer would be required to undergo Technical Training in all or any of the following Company designed courses as a specified programme. The curriculum may be modified based on the Company’s requirement from time to time. At present there are two modules (also called stream programmes) of technical training as enumerated below:

Generic Stream Programme: Programming principles, database concepts, designing concepts, quality systems concepts, computer hardware & software, network & system administration and other subjects.

Technology Specific Stream Programme: Open Source Technologies, Mobile Development, Web Development, ERP, Social, Data analytics, Cloud and other subjects.

Management Skills Training:

Every Engineer shall undergo pre-determined management skills programme during and as a part of the FT. These programmes would cover communication skills and business skills and such other components as the Company may determine from time to time depending upon varying business requirements of the Company.

It is mandatory that every Engineer qualifies in the manner specified both in Technical Training and Management Skills Training. All evaluation criteria and training process / methodology shall be exclusively determined by the Company and may be reviewed by the Company from time to time.
